## Purpose
A live pricing-negotiation framework for sales conversations with clinic owners. Companion to PRICING doc (answers its "how to negotiate live" open question, though dated one day earlier).

## Core stance
Goal is not to "quote a price" but to "anchor value, test willingness, and close with flexibility." Pricing conversations "determine whether you win deals or lose them."

## The 10-point framework
1. **Control conversation order**: never start with price. Order: understand their workflow → identify pain → link solution to that pain → then discuss pricing. If price comes too early they compare it to "zero (their current cost)" instead of the "problem cost".
2. **Anchor value before price**: e.g. "delays at reception and patient wait time seem to be your biggest issue... This system would help structure appointments, reduce congestion, and let doctors see their lineup ahead of time." Pricing becomes "a solution cost, not a software cost."
3. **Use a price range first, not a fixed number**: NOT "It costs ₦30,000" — instead "For clinics of your size, it usually falls between ₦20,000 and ₦50,000 monthly, depending on usage."
4. **Read their reaction**: "That's too expensive" → they don't see value or wrong tier; respond "That's fair—what would you expect something like this to cost?" (gets their price anchor). Neutral "Okay" → room to position higher tier. Quick "That's fine" → likely underpriced.
5. **"Pilot Close" (best early-stage strategy)**: "Since we're just rolling this out, we can start you on a pilot for 1–2 months at no cost, then move to a discounted early partner plan." Removes risk, increases adoption, gets commitment.
6. **Trade value, don't discount blindly**: "We can offer a reduced rate if you're willing to be an early partner and give feedback as we build." Trading lower price for strategic value (feedback, case study, referral).
7. **Tier positioning (psychology)**: mention higher tier first — "Some hospitals pay upwards of ₦80,000+, but for your clinic size you'd likely fall around ₦25,000–₦35,000."
8. **Always tie price to scale**: "The pricing is structured so smaller clinics aren't overcharged—it scales with how many patients you handle."
9. **Close with soft commitment**: not immediate payment — "If we set this up and it works as expected, would you be comfortable continuing on a monthly plan?" ("This is your real close.")
10. **Biggest advantage**: selling reduced waiting time, better patient experience, improved doctor efficiency, clinic organization — not software. Always return to "This improves how your clinic runs daily."

## Adaptable script (verbatim core)
"From what I've seen, a lot of clinics struggle with managing patient flow and keeping things organized, especially during busy hours. We're building a system that handles appointments, queues, and doctor scheduling in one place. For clinics your size, pricing typically falls between ₦20,000 and ₦50,000 monthly. But since we're onboarding early partners, we can start with a pilot and move to a discounted plan after testing."

## Final insight (decision on early strategy)
Early goal is NOT to maximize revenue or perfectly price; it is to "get clinics using your system and depending on it" — then pricing optimization becomes easy.
