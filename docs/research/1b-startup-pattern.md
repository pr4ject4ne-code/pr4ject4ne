## Purpose
Founder's year-by-year goal/roadmap note — despite the title, the content is not an analysis of a pattern but a 2-year goal list with a patient-volume plan.

> YEAR 1
> GOAL 1: OBTAIN 1ST PARTNER
> GOAL 2: PASS TRIAL
> GOAL 3: GET 1ST BIG GIG
> GOAL 4: HIT 1M A YEAR
> PLAN

> YEAR 2

## Decisions / commitments
- Go-to-market sequence: partner first → trial → "big gig" → revenue. Matches the hospital-partner validation strategy in "Walls of importance".
- Scale target: **100 hospitals x 50 patients/day = 5k patients/day = 150k/month** as the basis for hitting "1M a year".
- Year 2 pivot/expansion: "advance the private practice" — private clinics/practices are the year-2 growth vector (also matches Walls of importance's private-clinic opportunity).

## Key numbers
- 1M/year (year 1), 10M+/year (year 2) — currency unspecified (likely revenue; could be NGN or USD, unstated).
- 150K patients/month, 5k/day, 50 patients/day per hospital, 100 hospitals.

## Open questions
- Currency and whether 1M is revenue or patients-derived revenue; the implied per-patient monetization rate is not stated (1M/yr over 1.8M patients/yr ≈ 0.55 per patient if revenue).
- What "PASS TRIAL" concretely means (pilot acceptance criteria undefined).
- 100 hospitals in year 1 is aggressive vs "Walls of importance" advice of 2–5 pilot clinics — likely trial (2–5) then scale, but unreconciled.
