## Architecture: 4 views, accessed via different routes
1. **Personnel view** — only accessible from login if the account was added to the hospital through the administrative route. Subdivided into desk, sanitary, lab, nurse, doctor, each with different views per tasks/departments, with classes and subclasses, e.g. (resident, house officer, consultant) = doctor[class], radiology[subclass]. Each role view generally has two sections: **queue** (pertaining to patients directly) and **tasks** (not pertaining to patients directly).
   - **The Desk**: non-medical staff handling HMO, financial, customer service, transportation, security, clerk, liaison offices, human resources — each with role-specific dashboards.
   - **Sanitary**: coordinates the cleaning crew for efficiency/orderliness — "a hub that dishes out specific tasks by a roster from the admin".
   - **The Lab**: roster given by dashboard, subject to the designated admin.
   - Every dashboard has a **private calendar**; admins may add/remove only events they are designated for; general updates pending the admin.
   - **Doctor**: same principles but permits patient viewing — patient bio data and symptoms; shows queue, and patients are accessible from the queue through a mini window.
2. **Admin view** — for leadership roles (department heads, administrative staff in authority). Can: (1) send general messages/announcements, (2) plan and distribute tasks, (3) organize meetings, (4) update calendars, (5) monitor under-management. General accommodation for rescheduling, veto, and relevant data transfer between authorized personnel (read only).
3. **Customer/Patient view** — key features: appointment online; expected wait time and queue formation (2nd in line, next in line, 10th in line); booking consultant. Priority rights: patients can only book a consultant if their tally is not full and previous patients due for visit take priority (if indicated at least 3 days to consultation date). Labelled KEY NEGOTIATION FACTOR: benefits patient (less stress, more willingness to go to hospital); reduces hospital clutter and improves consulting order; encourages booking due to convenience. (Identical text to For Patient_260322.)
4. **Technical view** — named as the fourth view here but not elaborated in this doc; GEMINI NOTES explains it as a higher level letting the founder "monitor all the activities and fix issues while it runs".
