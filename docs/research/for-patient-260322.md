## Purpose
Defines the patient-facing feature set and the booking-priority rule; framed partly as a sales/negotiation argument to hospitals.

## Patient-facing features
1. Appointment online
2. Expected wait time and queue formation shown to patient ("2nd in line, next in line, 10th in line")
3. Booking a consultant

## Priority rights rule (operational)
Patients can only book a consultant if that consultant's tally (capacity) is not full, and previous patients already due for a visit take priority — provided the visit is indicated at least 3 days before the consultation date.

## Why it matters (labelled "KEY NEGOTIATION FACTOR")
1. Benefits patient — less stress, more willingness to go to hospital
2. Reduces hospital clutter and improves consulting order
3. Encourages patients to book appointments due to convenience
