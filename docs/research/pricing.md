## Purpose
Defines the pricing model for the clinic/hospital workflow SaaS. Opens with the founder's own framing, then a structured "field-ready pricing structure tailored for Nigerian private clinics" (reads like advisor/AI-assisted output the founder adopted).

## Founder's framing (page 1) — decision
"So were doing a 4 tier system":
1. **Build fee** — small one-off setup fee "to assure commitment (may be applicable if arrangements are previously 'terminated')"
2. **Basic subscription (licensing)** to use service
3. **Patient volume Scaling**
4. **Additional feature based ad-ons**
"I intend on using this to get to the total pricing."
Note: "for tier 2, a stipend method will be applied, charging each patient per payable visit ie networking fee" — i.e. a per-patient-visit networking fee layered onto the subscription.

## Guiding principles
Pricing must: feel affordable at first glance, scale with clinic growth, be easy to explain in <30 seconds, leave room for future increases.

## Recommended pricing table (Naira)
- **Tier 1 — Small Clinics (Starter)**: target 1–2 doctors, low patient flow. ₦15,000–₦25,000/month, up to 500 patients/month. Core features: appointment booking, patient registration, doctor dashboard, queue management.
- **Tier 2 — Growing Clinics**: target 2–5 doctors. ₦30,000–₦60,000/month, up to 1,500 patients/month. Everything in Tier 1 + admin dashboard, basic reporting, multi-staff support.
- **Tier 3 — Private Hospitals**: target larger clinics / small hospitals. ₦80,000–₦150,000+/month, up to 5,000 patients/month. Full admin controls, department-level dashboards, task management, internal communication tools.
- **Tier 4 — Enterprise / Custom**: target large hospitals. Custom pricing, unlimited/very high volume. Full system deployment, integrations, dedicated support, custom workflows.

## Volume scaling (add-on)
Blocks instead of per-patient: every extra 500 patients → ₦5,000–₦10,000. Keeps pricing predictable, non-intimidating, scalable.

## Build/setup fee
- Early stage: ₦0 for first 3–5 clinics (waived).
- Later: ₦50,000–₦150,000 depending on size, onboarding complexity, training required. Position as "onboarding & setup support".

## Add-on pricing (introduce only after product maturity)
- Telemedicine → ₦10k–₦30k/month
- Advanced analytics → ₦15k–₦50k/month
- Multi-branch → premium tier
- Priority support → premium

## Sales guidance
Do NOT present all tiers immediately. Start with: "We typically price based on clinic size. For most clinics like yours, it falls between ₦20,000 and ₦50,000 monthly." Adjust on their size and reaction.
Early pilot offer (marked "Very Important"): "We'll onboard you for free for 1–2 months, then place you on a discounted plan as an early partner." Example: normal ₦30,000 → pilot clinic pays ₦15,000. Builds goodwill, locks in early adopters, creates testimonials.

## Final recommendation (decision)
Start with 3 visible tiers, simple monthly pricing, soft volume caps, waived setup fee for early users. Refine after 5–10 paying clinics, real usage data, willingness-to-pay feedback.
